Privacy Policy

Introduction: This policy explains how Zero collects and uses your data. Data Collection: We collect your email address (for account creation) and audio recordings (Voice Notes).

Audio Data: Voice notes are transcribed into text. This text is processed to provide feedback on your understanding of educational content. AI Processing: We use third-party AI service providers (such as OpenAI) to analyze transcripts. Your data is not used by these providers to train their general models.

Data Retention: You may delete your account and all associated voice/text data at any time through the in-app settings. Security: We use industry-standard encryption to protect your audio files and transcripts.
